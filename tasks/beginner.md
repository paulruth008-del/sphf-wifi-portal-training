# Beginner Tasks

These tasks are suitable for first-time learners or short workshop sessions.

## Suggested sequence

1. Read `docs/overview.md`
2. Pick one learning track
3. Complete two beginner tasks in that track
4. Share one thing your track cares about that another track might miss

## Beginner challenge ideas

- Rewrite one portal message in clearer language
- Identify three placeholders in the repo
- Explain the captive portal flow in your own words
- Find one accessibility, data, security, or networking improvement
